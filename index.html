          How it works

          What happens each session.

          1. 1

            Move

            Drag or click a piece. Any legal move — we won't force you into a script.

          2. 2

            Verdict

            Two axes, plain English. TheoryA known move from established opening theory. status plus a quality read — Strong, Playable, Inaccuracy, Mistake, or Blunder — with the principle each move supports or breaks.

          3. 3

            Takeaway

            Finish all 15 moves and the recap names one rule worth remembering next game.

          Most players don't lose the opening because they failed to memorize — they lose it because they didn't understand what each early move was for.
