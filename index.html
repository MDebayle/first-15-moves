      How it works

      Five moves, five things to notice.

      1. 1

        Play a move

        Drag or click a piece. The board accepts any legal move — we won't force you into a script.

      2. 2

        Get a verdict

        Book, playable, inaccuracy, mistake, or blunder — based on theory and what Stockfish thinks.

      3. 3

        See the principles

        Each move is tagged with the ideas it supports or breaks: center, development, king safety, tempo.

      4. 4

        Finish 15 moves

        Complete the opening phase. The computer plays a real reply at each step, not a softball.

      5. 5

        Take a lesson away

        The recap names what you did well, where you drifted, and the one idea worth remembering next time.

      A good opening won't win every game, but a bad opening makes every game harder. Most players don't lose the opening because they failed to memorize — they lose it because they didn't understand what each early move was for.

      This site pairs Stockfish with hand-written theory notes. The engine checks the math. The coach explains the meaning.
